# CozyDreams App: Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Vision

CozyDreams is an interactive, dreamy web application where users curate and decorate their personal softcore-themed rooms, express themselves through avatars and journals, explore a gentle social community, and enjoy a soothing, emotionally comforting digital experience. The vision is to create a peaceful, safe, and uplifting online space emphasizing emotional design, gentle interaction, and delightful, whimsical visuals.

## Target Users

- Teens and young adults seeking a positive, friendly, and creative online environment.
- Individuals drawn to soft, cozy, pastel, and dreamy aesthetics.
- Users who enjoy self-expression, customization, and gentle social interactions.
- Anyone looking for a stress-relief escape, mood tracking, or calming digital activities.

## User Experience Goals

- Foster emotional comfort, safety, and self-expression.
- Deliver a whimsical, soft pastel, round-edged interface with floating elements, animations, and gentle transitions.
- Minimize cognitive load; interactions are intuitive, feedback is encouraging, errors never harsh.
- Subtle, soothing background music and gentle UI sounds enhance the mood.
- Empathy-focused: daily encouragements, anonymous kind notes, subtle social features.

## Core Features Overview

- **Room Decorator**: Drag-and-drop room items, ambient lighting, animated backgrounds, and layers for a vivid, customizable environment.
- **Avatar Customizer**: Deep options for moods, outfits, poses to reflect self-expression and current feelings.
- **Journaling**: Daily prompt with text and audio entry; privacy emphasized. Users unlock decor through kind actions or regular journaling.
- **Social Exploration**: Visit other rooms via a scrollable grid; leave and receive anonymous kind notes.
- **Rewards System**: Collect Hearts and Stars as rewards for journaling and kindness.
- **Shop**: Spend earned rewards on wallpapers, decorations, and avatar extras with instant, animated previews.
- **Profile Customization**: Select avatar, ‘vibe’, scent, playlists, and pin meaningful quotes to your room’s wall.
- **Music & Sound**: Soothing lofi/ambient soundtracks and playlists with simple controls.
- **Daily Interactions**: Login rewards, daily hugs, encouraging message “whisper bubble.”
- **Secret Room Guests**: Occasional mystical creatures visit to drop gifts.
- **Navigation & Layout**: Fluid bottom tab navigation, each screen presented in floating pastel frames.

## Success Criteria

- Users feel calmer and happier after sessions.
- Room and avatar customization induce pride and creative engagement.
- Social actions (notes, visits) are frequent and positive with zero harassment/toxicity.
- Technical performance: snappy, smooth UI transitions.
- High retention for daily journaling and login rewards.

## Non-Goals

- No harsh feedback, competitive rankings, or aggressive monetization
- No text-based public chat; interactions are always soft, safe, and gentle.

## Alignment and Inspiration

CozyDreams draws inspiration from Animal Crossing, Pinterest, and journaling apps, but reimagined in a digital, emotionally nurturing, softcore pastel space.
